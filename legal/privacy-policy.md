## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the My Buds M8 mobile application (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

---

## 2. Information We Access and Process

### A. Location Data (Earbuds Last Seen)

With your permission, the App accesses your device's location to save the last known location of your earbuds when they disconnect, and to show your current position on the map while the "Find Earbuds" screen is open.

- **Storage:** This location data (latitude, longitude, and timestamp) is stored **exclusively on your local device** within the App's private storage (DataStore).
- **No Upload:** We do **not** transmit, upload, or share this location data with us or any third parties. It never leaves your phone.
- **Control:** You can clear this saved data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android system settings or by disabling the "Track earbuds location" toggle in the App's settings.

### B. Bluetooth Data

The App requires Bluetooth access to connect to and manage your earbuds (battery monitoring, firmware updates, noise control). This is used solely for communication with your paired audio devices.

### C. Notifications

If enabled, the App uses notifications to display battery status and noise control options in your notification shade. This is a local function and does not involve sending data externally.

### D. Crash Logs (Optional)

If the App crashes, it may generate a local log file to help you report the issue. This log is stored locally and is only shared if you explicitly choose to send it to us for support purposes.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the accessed data strictly for the following purposes:

- **Core Functionality:** To connect to your earbuds, display battery levels, adjust settings, and save the last known location for the "Find Earbuds" feature.
- **Improvement:** To diagnose crashes (only if you choose to share the log).

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

**We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.** Because the location data is stored locally on your device, we have no access to it. We do not share any data with third-party advertisers or analytics companies.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Location:** Retained on your device until you delete it. You can delete it by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or by using the in-app "Track earbuds location" toggle.
- **Crash Logs:** Retained locally until you clear them or uninstall the App.

---

## 6. Permissions Explained

- **Location (Fine / Coarse / Background):** Required to save the "last seen" location of your earbuds when they disconnect, so you can find them later. Background location is used only if you enable the "Track earbuds location" feature, so the App can save the location even if the App is closed. Bluetooth scanning on modern Android does not require location access.
- **Bluetooth (Connect / Scan):** Required to communicate with your earbuds.
- **Notifications:** Required to show the persistent battery and ANC controls.
